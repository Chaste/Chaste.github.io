## Chaste team

Please note: For general questions about Chaste, for example on installation or set-up, please email the users mailing list, rather than choosing someone from the lists below to email. To subscribe to the list please [follow these instructions](https://web.maillist.ox.ac.uk/ox/subscribe/chaste-users). You can then send an email with your question to [chaste-users@maillist.ox.ac.uk](mailto:chaste-users@maillist.ox.ac.uk).
