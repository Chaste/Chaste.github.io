Privacy is important to us.
We aim to collect the minimum amount of information necessary to understand how our website is used, while respecting the privacy of our visitors.

### Website analytics

We use **[Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/)** to understand overall website usage, such as which pages are visited and how visitors arrive at the site.
Plausible is a privacy-focused analytics platform that does **not** use cookies and does **not** collect information that directly identifies individuals.

The analytics data we receive is aggregated and used only to understand general trends in website usage.
We do not track individual users or create user profiles.

For full details about Plausible, please refer to:

* Plausible Privacy Policy: [https://plausible.io/privacy](https://plausible.io/privacy)
* Plausible Data Policy: [https://plausible.io/data-policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy)

### Transparency

To be fully transparent, we provide a public view of the analytics data available to us at: [https://chaste.github.io/analytics](/analytics).

This page shows the same aggregated statistics that we see.

### Contact

If you have questions about how analytics are used on this website, please contact us via our [Q&A discussions forum](https://github.com/Chaste/Chaste/discussions).
