We are pleased to announce an upcoming workshop on [Chaste](https://chaste.github.io).

Details will be coming soon.


## Event Details


### When & Where

The workshop will be held at the **University of Sheffield** from **Wed 1st - Fri 3rd July**. Most activities will take place in [Pam Liversidge Building](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Pam+Liversidge+Building/@53.382714,-1.4802816,688m/data=!3m3!1e3!4b1!5s0x487978802351bc8f:0x30731602a8415b5f!4m6!3m5!1s0x487978802cec084b:0x60e5580cdf19b137!8m2!3d53.3827108!4d-1.4777067!16s%2Fg%2F1pxwp_ysp?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDMxMS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), Design Studio 02 - E05.



## Workshop Aims

Over the course of the workshop, you will:
- Learn about how to use chaste for cell-based modelling
- Hear about the latest software features
- Have opportunities to network with other researchers in the cell-based modelling sphere throughout the workshop and at the free workshop dinner on the Thursday evening
- Get personalised help with how you might be able to integrate chaste with your work and contribute back to the project

### Transport Links

#### By Train
The University is easily accessible by train via **Sheffield Station**. 

The university is around a **25 minute walk** from the station. The hotel is slightly further, around a **40 minute walk** from the station. The walk is generally up hill, so if you prefer, both the blue and yellow tram routes go from the Sheffield Station / Hallam University tram stop to the University of Sheffield tram stop. Tram tickets can be purchased on board from the conductor (card payment accepted), or ahead of time through the Travel South Yorkshire App.

Buses are easily accessible from the [Sheffield Interchange](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Sheffield+Interchange/@53.3782779,-1.4677189,787m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m23!1m16!4m15!1m6!1m2!1s0x4879829cc4a16fe7:0xe072f692e5badf1c!2sSheffield+station,+Sheaf+St,+Sheffield+City+Centre,+Sheffield+S1+2BP!2m2!1d-1.4624034!2d53.3782342!1m6!1m2!1s0x4879827419ed2795:0xf200c908b1596709!2sThe+Rutland+Hotel,+452+Glossop+Rd,+Sheffield+S10+2PY!2m2!1d-1.4963062!2d53.3768812!3e2!3m5!1s0x48798282b3cd6939:0xf769eaa9ccbfb382!8m2!3d53.3809534!4d-1.4645763!16s%2Fg%2F11gh2zf639?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDMyMi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) a few minutes from the station. **Black cab taxis** and **Ubers** are both available from the taxi rank at the station.

#### By Car
The hotel has a first-come first-served car park available at £10/night.

There are numerous car parks in the city centre, though parking for multiple days can be expensive. The closest car park to the venue is the Q-Park Rockingham Street.
